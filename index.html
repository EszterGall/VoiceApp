VOICE COMMANDS

Just say what you need.

“Read this”Start reading the text
“Pause”Pause the reading
“Continue”Resume reading
“Repeat”Read it again
“Read slower”Lower the speaking speed
“Read faster”Increase the speaking speed
“Stop”Stop the current reading

Voice recognition depends on browser and device support. The on-screen controls remain available as an alternative.
